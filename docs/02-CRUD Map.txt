# CRUD Map: NutriTrack

(Based only on what is in the NutriTrack zip: `nutritrack_complete (1).sql`, `app.py` (its routes), and the templates `dashboard.html`, `children.html`, `assessments.html`, `feeding.html`, `followup.html`, `base.html`.)

## Table CRUD Operations

| Table | C | R | U | D | Pages | Who |
|-------|---|---|---|---|-------|-----|
| barangay | – | ? | – | – | children.html (dropdown) | Staff (BNS / Health Worker): R |
| user_account | – | ? | – | – | assessments.html, followup.html (Recorded by dropdown, active users only) | Staff (BNS / Health Worker): R |
| child | ? | ? | ? | ? | children.html, dashboard.html, feeding.html, followup.html | Staff (BNS / Health Worker): all |
| guardian | ? | ? | – | – | children.html (create form), followup.html (contacts) | Staff (BNS / Health Worker): C, R |
| child_guardian | ? | ? | – | – | children.html (create form), followup.html (contacts) | Staff (BNS / Health Worker): C, R |
| nutrition_status | – | ? | – | – | dashboard.html | Staff (BNS / Health Worker): R |
| assessment | ? | ? | – | ? | assessments.html, dashboard.html, followup.html (optional dropdown) | Staff (BNS / Health Worker): C, R, D |
| measurement | ? | ? | – | – | assessments.html | Staff (BNS / Health Worker): C, R (saved together with the assessment) |
| feeding_program | – | ? | – | – | feeding.html | Staff (BNS / Health Worker): R |
| feeding_attendance | ? | ? | – | ? | feeding.html | Staff (BNS / Health Worker): C, R, D |
| follow_up | ? | ? | – | ? | followup.html | Staff (BNS / Health Worker): C, R, D |

**Notes:**
- barangay: No create/update/delete - single seeded row used as a dropdown. child, user_account and feeding_program point to it with ON DELETE RESTRICT.
- user_account: No create/update/delete - accounts are seeded and only listed in the "Recorded by" dropdown (app.py only loads rows with `is_active = TRUE`). app.py has no login or role checks. assessment and follow_up point to it with RESTRICT.
- child: Delete exists, but it is rejected by ON DELETE RESTRICT when the child already has assessments, attendance or follow-ups (the page says so).
- guardian: No update/delete - guardian is created together with the child. Editing a child does not show the guardian fields. Linked through child_guardian with RESTRICT.
- child_guardian: No update/delete - created with the child. Removed automatically when the child is deleted (ON DELETE CASCADE).
- nutrition_status: Read only - fixed lookup list seeded in the database.
- assessment: No update - app.py has `/assessments/add` and `/assessments/<aid>/delete` but no update route. Delete cascades to measurement, and sets follow_up.assessment_id to NULL (SET NULL). The assessments page also has a per-child history view (read only).
- measurement: Created in the same transaction as its assessment (weight, height, MUAC) and shown in the assessment history table. No update/delete of its own - it is removed by CASCADE when its assessment is deleted.
- feeding_program: Read only - no create form in the zip, programs come from seed data. Deleting one would CASCADE to feeding_attendance.
- feeding_attendance: No update - a wrong row is deleted and re-added.
- follow_up: No update - there is no edit form, only add and delete.

## Form Field Lists

### child table (children.html)

| Column name | Form control | Required? |
|-------------|--------------|-----------|
| child_id | none (CHAR(36), generated by the system) | – |
| barangay_id | `<select name="barangay_id">` (shows barangay, stores barangay_id) | Yes |
| first_name | `<input name="first_name" maxlength="80">` | Yes |
| middle_name | `<input name="middle_name" maxlength="80">` | No |
| last_name | `<input name="last_name" maxlength="80">` | Yes |
| birth_date | `<input type="date" name="birth_date">` | Yes |
| sex | `<select name="sex">` (M, F) | Yes |
| address | `<input name="address" maxlength="255">` | Yes |
| created_at | none (DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP) | – |

### assessment table (assessments.html, "New assessment" form; saved together with measurement by `/assessments/add`)

The form has `novalidate`, so the browser does not enforce anything. The database NOT NULL / CHECK / UNIQUE rules decide what is accepted. "Required?" below follows the database.

| Column name | Form control | Required? |
|-------------|--------------|-----------|
| assessment_id | none (CHAR(36), generated by the system) | – |
| child_id | `<select name="child_id">` (shows child name, stores child_id) | Yes |
| recorded_by | `<select name="recorded_by">` (shows "username (role)", stores user_id) | Yes |
| status_id | `<select name="status_id">` (shows status_name, stores status_id) | Yes |
| assessment_date | `<input type="date" name="assessment_date">` (one assessment per child per date) | Yes |
| notes | `<textarea name="notes" rows="2">` | No |
| weight_kg (measurement) | `<input type="number" step="0.01" name="weight_kg">` (DB rule: > 0 and ? 200) | Yes |
| height_cm (measurement) | `<input type="number" step="0.01" name="height_cm">` (DB rule: > 0 and ? 250) | Yes |
| muac_cm (measurement) | `<input type="number" step="0.01" name="muac_cm">` (optional, 0–100) | No |

Extra on this form: a suggestion box that reads the child's age and MUAC and suggests a nutrition status (NORMAL or WASTED). It is only a hint, and the health worker picks the status.
